Privacy Policy

SOMEWHERE game, version 1.0 (iPhone and Android) · Last updated: 15 October 2026

SOMEWHERE ("the game") is developed and published by Ajwad Daiki, an independent developer (private individual) based in France, who is the controller of the processing described here ("we"). For any question: daiki.ajwad@gmail.com.

The French version of this policy (confidentialite.html) says the same thing.

1. In short

  • No account: we never ask for your name, e-mail or age.
  • This version contains no ads and no purchases, no audience analytics and no trackers.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. The game plays offline.
  • Only one thing is sent automatically: technical crash reports, through the Unity engine, which contain neither your name nor your e-mail.
  • Your game log only leaves your phone if you send it yourself by e-mail.

2. What stays on your phone

The game stores, on your device only:

  • your progress (Courage level, coins, gems, items, outfits, Tree, unlocked Nights…);
  • your settings (sound, vibration, display, joystick);
  • the "Nights log": one line per Night played (see section 4).

These files are not sent to us. They may be included in your device backups (iCloud, Google backup) depending on your own settings: those backups are managed by Apple or Google, not by us. Uninstalling the game deletes everything (progress, settings, log).

3. Crash reports (Unity engine)

The game is built with the Unity engine, made by Unity Technologies. When the game crashes or hits a serious error, and the phone is online, a technical report is sent to Unity.

  • What it contains: the kind of error and its technical trace, the device model, the OS version, the game version, stability and performance information (memory, for example), the date, and a random technical identifier created by the engine to group reports. As with any connection, the IP address is visible to the servers receiving the report.
  • What it does not contain: no name, no e-mail, no advertising identifier, no location, no content of your games. We cannot link a report to a person.
  • Why: to find and fix crashes. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing a game that works (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This data is never used for advertising or tracking.
  • Who receives it: Unity Technologies, which shows it to us and may use it to fix its engine, under its own policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. Unity may process this data outside the European Union, including in the United States, with the safeguards required by the GDPR. Unity keeps it for a limited time; we do not export it anywhere else.
  • Reports are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). This version of the game has no setting to turn them off; they are only sent when a crash happens.

4. The Nights log and the "Send my log" button

At the end of each Night, the game adds one line to a local file (journal_des_nuits.jsonl). It describes the game: date and time, game mode, World, Night and its tier, the outcome (dawn, fall or return), time survived, area reached, what defeated the hero, the hero's level, styles and powers, your Courage level and, depending on the game version, your Might, your in-game currency balances, the difficulty settings in use and the game version. This file contains no name, no e-mail, no device identifier and no location.

It is used to balance the game's difficulty. It only leaves your phone if you decide so: the "Send my log" button opens your e-mail app (or your phone's share menu) with the file attached. Nothing is sent until you tap "Send" yourself, and you can read the file or cancel first.

If you send it, we receive the file, your e-mail address and whatever you write. We use them only to tune the game and to reply to you (legal basis: your voluntary action and our legitimate interest in improving the game). We keep them for 12 months at most, then delete them; you can ask us to delete them earlier at any time. They are stored in our mailbox, hosted by our e-mail provider.

5. If you write to us

If you contact us (help, bug, question), we receive your e-mail address and your message. We use them only to reply, and delete them no later than 12 months after our last exchange.

6. What the game does not do

  • No account, no social network login, no online save.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases in this version.
  • No analytics, no advertising identifier, no tracking across apps.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No chat between players.
  • We do not sell or rent any data.

7. Age

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country (15 in France), ask a parent before writing to us or sending us your log.

8. Your rights

For the data we hold (e-mails and logs you sent us), you can ask to access, correct or delete it, to restrict its use or to object: write to daiki.ajwad@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

Crash reports cannot identify you: we cannot find them from your name or e-mail. For what Unity processes on its side, see its policy (link in section 3).

To erase what is on your phone: uninstall the game.

If you think your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, CNIL (www.cnil.fr), or with the authority of your own country.

9. Changes

If the game changes how it handles data (for example when ads arrive), this page will be updated before that version is released, with its new date at the top.
