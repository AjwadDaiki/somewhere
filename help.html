Help

SOMEWHERE, version 1.0 · iPhone and Android · The game is in French for now; menu names are given in French with their meaning.

How to play

  1. Put your thumb anywhere and slide: the hero follows. A quick flick of the thumb and he leaps.
  2. Your moves strike on their own. Your job is positioning: don't stay in the crowd, circle around.
  3. Each level up, pick a card: a new style, an upgrade, a power.
  4. Your power recharges: the round badge under your level bubble shows when it's ready.
  5. A Night is three areas and their guardian, about six minutes. Hold on till dawn.
  6. If you fall, you lose your build, not your Bedroom: what you earned waits there so you come back stronger.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my save?

On your phone, and only there. The game saves by itself; there is no account and no online save. You can play offline.

Careful: uninstalling the game deletes your progress.

I'm changing phones: do I keep my progress?

This version has no built-in transfer. Your progress can come along if you copy your whole phone:

  • iPhone to iPhone: direct transfer ("Quick Start") or restoring an iCloud or computer backup.
  • Android to Android: Google backup, if it was turned on on the old phone. It depends on the brand and settings: not guaranteed.
  • iPhone to Android (or the other way): progress does not carry over.

Keep the old phone until you've checked that your Bedroom is there on the new one.

I can't hear anything
  • In the game: Réglages (Settings, the gear) → Le son (Sound) → turn up Musique (Music) and Sons (Sounds).
  • On iPhone: check the silent switch on the side. In silent mode, the game is quiet.
  • Turn the volume up with the phone's buttons while the game is open (that's the media volume, not the ringer).
  • Check that no Bluetooth headphones or speaker are connected without you noticing.
The game lags, gets hot or drains the battery
  • Réglages (Settings) → L'écran (Screen) → Images par seconde (Frames per second): pick 30. That's the power-saving mode: less heat, longer battery.
  • Close other open apps, then restart the game.
  • Your phone's low power mode can also slow the game down: that's normal.
  • A hot phone slows itself down to stay safe: let it cool for a few minutes.

If the game stays slow, write to us with your phone model: it helps us improve it.

Report a bug

Write to daiki.ajwad@gmail.com with:

  • your phone model and its version (iOS or Android);
  • the game version (in the Settings, or on the game's App Store or Google Play page);
  • what you were doing, what happened, and whether it happens again;
  • a screenshot or video if you can.

If your version of the game has the "Envoyer mon journal" (Send my log) button in the Settings, use it: it prepares an e-mail with how your last Nights went (no name, nothing that identifies you). Nothing is sent until you tap "Send". Details in the privacy policy.

If the game crashes, a technical report (without your name or e-mail) is sent automatically to help us fix it: see what it contains.

Delete my data

Uninstall the game: your progress, settings and Nights log are erased from the phone. The game has no account, so there is nothing else to close.

If you sent us an e-mail or your log and want it erased, write to us: done within one month.
